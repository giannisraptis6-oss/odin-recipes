- OMELET RECIPES -

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 30 ml milk (regular or vegan)
  • ½ green pepper (diced)
  • 6 cherry tomatoes (or grated tomato)
  • 1 tbsp parsley (chopped)
  • 2 slices turkey (cut into strips)
  • 40 gr yellow cheese (grated)
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

After we have all we need, proceed with the tasks to make our omelet:

  1. Preheat air fryer to 175°C with the pan inside.
  2. Prepare ingredients ready to mix with eggs.
  3. Beat eggs lightly with fork, add all ingredients, salt & pepper.
  4. Grease pan lightly with oil, pour in egg mixture.
  5. Place pan in air fryer, bake 12 min at 175°C.
  6. Remove & let cool slightly (it puffs up while baking).
